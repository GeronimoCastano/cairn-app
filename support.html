Your email and message are processed by Formspree and received by Cairn’s developer to answer your request. They are not posted publicly or used for marketing. Support privacy.

Before you report a problem

Playback or source connection

Try a different title and check whether the source works in another player on the same network. If only Cairn fails, tell us your source type (M3U, Xtream, Plex, or a file), device and OS, app version, and the exact error. Leave out the source address and credentials.

Casting or offline viewing

For casting, include the receiver model and confirm that both devices use the same Wi-Fi network and Local Network access is allowed. For downloads, tell us whether the problem is saving the video or playing a saved title.

Pricing and purchases

Purchases are not live yet. Cairn is planned as a free player with an optional one-time Pro upgrade across your supported Apple devices. The final price and feature split will be announced with the release.

Library materials

For LGPL source and relink materials, include your app version, build number, and platform. Requests are free.
