When playback doesn't start

Check that your source is reachable and that your subscription or access is valid. Try another title from the same source. Cairn cannot repair a source server or play DRM-protected streams.

When casting doesn't connect

Keep the phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi network and allow Local Network access for Cairn in system settings. Keep Cairn running on the phone while casting.

What to include in a report

  • Your device, OS version, and Cairn version from Settings.
  • What you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce it.
  • A screenshot or error message with private information removed.

Reports are public. Never include passwords, API keys, source tokens, full playlist URLs, or personal viewing history.

Purchases
