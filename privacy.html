How Cairn handles your library, connections, and support requests.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Cairn is a video player for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app does not automatically send your library or viewing history to the developer. Information you choose to send through support is separate.

On your deviceConnectionsWebsite & support

What stays on your device

  • Your sources: the servers and playlists you add. Passwords and API keys are kept in the Keychain on your device.
  • Your library and history: what you watched, where you stopped, favorites, and settings. The library and watch history are stored encrypted on your device.
  • Offline titles: videos you save to watch offline, stored in Cairn's folder on your device.

Removing a source from within Cairn is different from hiding it: hidden sources retain their data. App files and Keychain credentials are stored separately; deleting the app may leave Keychain entries behind. On Mac, Settings → Open local data folder shows where the app stores its local files. Contact support if you need help removing data.

What Cairn connects to

Cairn connects to services to load your content, enrich your library, and check your connection:

  • Your sources. Cairn connects directly to the servers and addresses you add, including any TV guide address, to load your library and play it. What those servers do with your requests is up to whoever runs them.
  • OpenSubtitles (opensubtitles.org or opensubtitles.com), when you search for subtitles. Cairn sends the title, its year, season and episode, and the languages you chose, plus your API key if you entered one.
  • TVmaze (tvmaze.com), to fill in episode details for series. Cairn sends the series name.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org), to load artwork for some titles.
  • ipinfo.io, for the VPN check. On startup and when your network changes, Cairn asks ipinfo.io which internet address your connection has, so it can show whether a VPN is on. The answer stays on your device.
  • YouTube, only when you choose to watch a trailer; it opens in your browser or the YouTube app.
  • TVs on your network, when you cast. The video goes from your iPhone or iPad straight to the TV over your local network.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's internet address when Cairn contacts them. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive.

Children

Cairn is not directed at children. The app does not automatically send personal data to the developer.

Website and support

The public Cairn website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may receive technical request information, including IP addresses, when you visit. Cairn does not add analytics or advertising to the site or set its own cookies.

The private support form is hosted by Tally. When the embedded form loads, Tally receives technical request information, such as your IP address and browser information. If you submit it, Tally processes your email address, topic, message, and any optional device, OS, and app-version details. Geronimo Castaño receives these answers in the Tally dashboard and in email notifications handled by his email provider, Microsoft. They are used to answer your request, not published or used for marketing. Tally’s own privacy policy applies to its service.

Support submissions and email copies are retained until manually deleted; there is currently no automatic deletion schedule. You can request correction or deletion through the support form. Dashboard entries and email copies are separate, so deleting one does not delete the other. Please do not submit passwords, source credentials, private playlist URLs, viewing history, or other sensitive information.

Public GitHub issues remain available for non-private reports. Their contents are received by the developer and GitHub and can be read by anyone. A GitHub account is required.

Planned purchases

Cairn does not currently offer in-app purchases. The planned Pro unlock will use Apple's in-app purchase system. Apple will process payment under its own privacy policy; the developer will not receive your payment-card details. This policy will be updated when purchases are available.
