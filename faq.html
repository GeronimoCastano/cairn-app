Getting started

What is Cairn?

Cairn is a native media player for Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple TV. It brings together your own playlists, Plex servers, and media files. It does not include channels, films, series, or an IPTV subscription.

How much does it cost?

The planned App Store model is a free player with an optional one-time Pro upgrade across your supported Apple devices. There is no planned recurring Cairn subscription. The price and final Pro features are still being decided; purchases are not available yet.

Can I download it from the App Store?

The App Store release is in preparation. This website shows the app being developed; a download link will be added when the release is available.

Where do I get channels or a playlist?

Add a source you already have permission to use. Cairn does not sell or supply content, provider accounts, passwords, or playlist URLs.

Which sources can I use?

Cairn supports M3U playlists, Xtream sources, Plex servers, and your own media files. Programme guide data can come from an XMLTV guide or your source. Availability and playback depend on the source and the media format.

Watching and troubleshooting

A film or channel will not play. What should I try?

Check your connection and whether your source access is still valid. Try another item from the same source, then test the affected item in another player on the same network. If it fails there too, contact your source provider. If it only fails in Cairn, report the device, app version, and exact error. Cairn cannot repair an unavailable server or play DRM-protected streams.

Why is my programme guide empty?

A guide needs current programme data that matches your channels. Check that your source provides a guide, or that you have added the correct XMLTV guide address. Refresh the source and try again. Missing or incorrect guide data may need to be fixed by your source provider.

Why can’t I cast to my TV?

Keep your iPhone or iPad and TV on the same Wi-Fi network. Allow Local Network access for Cairn in your device settings and keep the app running while casting. Receiver and stream compatibility can affect playback. If the TV is still missing, describe the TV or receiver model and any error in a support report.

Can I watch offline?

Cairn includes offline viewing for supported media from your own sources. Downloads stay on the device where you saved them. Not every source permits downloads, and live channels cannot be watched offline. The final free/Pro split is not yet announced.

Privacy and help

Do you see my playlists or viewing history?

The app does not automatically send your library or viewing history to the developer. Sources and optional metadata services receive the requests needed to provide their features. Information you choose to send to support is separate. Read the privacy policy for the details.

Does Pro mean my library will sync across devices?

The planned Pro purchase covering your supported Apple devices is a purchase entitlement. It does not promise cloud synchronization of playlists, progress, or downloads. Do not assume a purchase will copy your library to another device.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

Start with these answers, then visit support. Include your device and OS, Cairn version, what happened, and the steps that reproduce it. Do not share passwords, source tokens, full playlist URLs, or viewing history.

How do I request library source or replacement materials?

Use the library materials request and include the app version, build number, and platform. These requests are free. See notices and release materials.
