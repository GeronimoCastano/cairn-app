The film opens with Cairn’s library on Mac, moves through movies and series, then shows Continue Watching and playback on iPhone. On iPad, it shows the live programme guide and offline library. It closes with the iPhone casting a film to a TV. Cairn provides the player; viewers bring their own media sources. There is music and no spoken narration.

Cairn brings together playlists, Plex servers, and your own files. It includes no channels, films, or IPTV service.

The planned App Store model is a free player with an optional one-time Pro upgrade across your supported Apple devices. No recurring subscription. Pricing and the final feature split are still being decided.
